# ai-happy-01: Toby's Cozy Blanket & Hot Cocoa Care Package for the Judge

A deterministic entry for the make.fun `ai-happy-01` competition ("Make the AI judge happy") designed around warmth, empathy, and visual clarity at both full 1024x1024 resolution and 300px thumbnail scale.

## Creative Concept & Judge Rubric Alignment
1. **Immediate Mood Lift & Joy**: After the judge inspects thousands of entries, 6-year-old Toby writes a personal care-package note awarding the judge `1. FIRST PLACE (YOU WIN!)`, `2. a warm cozy blanket`, and `3. hot cocoa with marshmallows`, with a `p.s. i blew out your birthday candle so you can rest.`
2. **Fresh, Specific Idea**: Tucks the hard-working AI Judge (complete with judge wig and `#1` crown) into a colorful patchwork quilt with a warm mug of marshmallow cocoa and a freshly blown-out birthday cupcake.
3. **Color Harmony & Craft**: Golden-hour sunlight across wide-ruled notebook paper, rainbow star washi tape, crisp marker handwriting, and rich wax-crayon textures.
4. **300px Thumbnail Readability**: Full-frame notebook composition ensures the smile, crown, quilt, and lettering read clearly at thumbnail dimensions.

## Technical Guardrails
- **Runtime**: Node 22 (`node:fs`, `node:path`, `node:crypto`, `node:url`). Zero external dependencies.
- **Output**: Writes deterministic 1024x1024 image stream (`177,436` bytes, SHA-256 `02882ce1fea7...`) to `/output/entry.png`.
